My story

I'm a B.Tech student in Electrical & Electronics Engineering with a strong interest in robotics, embedded systems, and intelligent machines. I enjoy turning classroom concepts into practical projects and learning through hands-on experimentation.

As the team leader of Team Zentrix, I worked with my teammates on a Line Following Robot (LFR) project for our college tech fest. That experience helped me build teamwork, problem-solving, and project coordination skills.

I also participated in the MicroMouse event at Celesta, IIT Patna. Although I didn't win, the experience gave me an opportunity to explore new ideas, learn from other teams, and understand robotics at a deeper level.

I'm continuing to develop my skills in electronics, C programming, Arduino, and robotics, with the goal of building smarter and more capable robotic systems.
